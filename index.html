Overview

About the Course

AP Research is the second course in the College Board AP Capstone program. Students identify a research question, survey the existing literature, design a methodology, collect and analyze data, and produce a formal academic paper and oral defense — work equivalent to an undergraduate thesis seminar.

Willie co-teaches sections of the course where students are pursuing quantitative, computational, or mixed-methods research. He supports literature review in technical domains, methodology design for data-driven studies, statistical analysis, and computational tools for data collection and processing.

  • Formulate a focused, researchable academic question
  • Conduct a systematic literature review and synthesize sources
  • Design an appropriate research methodology and collect primary data
  • Analyze findings using quantitative or qualitative methods
  • Write and defend a formal academic research paper

Curriculum

Course Phases

Phase 1

Research Design

Question formation, narrowing scope, identifying a gap, and planning the methodology.

Phase 2

Literature Review

Systematic search, source evaluation, annotation, and synthesizing prior research.

Phase 3

Quantitative & Computational Methods

Data collection, statistical analysis, Python/R for processing, and visualization.

Phase 4

Analysis & Interpretation

Drawing conclusions, addressing limitations, considering alternative interpretations.

Phase 5

Academic Writing

IMRD structure, academic voice, citation, and revision toward the final paper.

Phase 6

Oral Defense

Presentation design, Q&A preparation, and communicating research to an academic panel.
