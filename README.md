# AP Research
### The Cushman School — Willie Avendano (Co-Taught)

AP Research is the second course in the College Board AP Capstone program. Students design and conduct independent research projects, producing a 4,000–5,000 word academic paper and a 15–20 minute oral defense.

Willie co-teaches this course, supporting students whose research involves quantitative methods, data science, statistical modeling, and computational approaches to complex questions.

## Student Research Projects

Projects below apply data science and NLP techniques to open questions in social science, economics, and finance.

### [Political Polarity Text Analyzer](https://github.com/willieavendano/political-polarity)
NLP pipeline for measuring political polarity in text corpora. Applies TF-IDF features, logistic regression, and fine-tuned transformer models (DistilBERT) to classify political lean in news articles.

Relevant research questions: *How do algorithmic systems encode political bias? What are the ethical implications of automated media classification?*

**Stack:** Python, scikit-learn, HuggingFace Transformers, Gradio

---

### [Stock Price Forecaster](https://github.com/willieavendano/stock-forecast)
Browser-based forecasting tool implementing LSTM, Geometric Brownian Motion, and Decision Tree regressors — all running client-side via TensorFlow.js. Produces uncertainty bands and cross-model ensemble forecasts.

Relevant research questions: *How well do machine learning models capture financial time series? What are the limits of quantitative prediction in efficient markets?*

**Stack:** React, TensorFlow.js, Recharts, Vite
